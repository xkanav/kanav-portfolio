The Crisis

Why existing credit fails
India's farmers

India has robust farming credit schemes on paper — but deep structural gaps leave millions trapped between moneylenders and debt cycles.

"Loans intended for productivity often get diverted to meet immediate social obligations. The loans get renewed every year without actual repayment." — Thomas Franco, All India Bank Officers' Federation
"Farming barely pays enough to sustain a family. If there's a medical emergency or a wedding, the pressure is too much." — Mohammad Mehraj, Village Head, Uttar Pradesh

The KCC scheme has disbursed over $120Bn to farmers by 2024, but a 2024 study found only a fraction reaches agriculture: 28% of KCC loans were used for household needs, 22% for medical expenses, and 14% for education — because the farm income itself is too volatile to absorb shocks.

Our Approach

KrishiSetu: An intelligent
credit infrastructure

"Replace collateral with agronomic intelligence. Replace rigid EMIs with harvest-aligned repayment. Replace exclusion with inclusion."
KrishiSetu is a B2B2C agri-fintech platform that sits between banks/NBFCs and farmers. We build a proprietary Agri-Credit Score powered by weather data, crop yield history, satellite NDVI, MSP prices, and government scheme enrollment — then offer lenders a pre-underwritten loan recommendation aligned to each crop's natural cash-flow cycle.

Farmers interact via an IVR phone line (no smartphone needed), a multilingual app, or through a network of trained KrishiMitras (village-level agents). Repayment is tied to post-harvest income, not calendar dates.
🧠
Agri-Credit Scoring Engine
AI model ingesting 11 data layers: rainfall (IMD), crop type, NDVI satellite yield, district soil health, MSP & market prices, land records, PM-KISAN disbursement history, PMFBY enrollment, and informal transaction patterns via Business Correspondent networks.
🌱
Crop-Cycle Aligned Credit
Loan disbursement at sowing season; repayment scheduled 14–21 days post-harvest. No penalty for weather delays — IMD-verified drought or flood triggers a 90-day moratorium automatically, backed by PMFBY payouts as collateral alternative.
☂️
Parametric Insurance Wrapper
Partnership with a dedicated agri-insurer offering parametric triggers (weather index, satellite imagery). Integrated with PMFBY. Insurance premium is part of loan package — claim auto-settles loan default, removing risk from both farmer and lender.
📡
No-Tech Access Layer
IVR in 12 languages for zero-literacy farmers. KrishiMitras (trained local agents) as last-mile touchpoint. Aadhaar eKYC + mKisan SMS for disbursement. Partnerships with CSCs (Common Service Centres) across 6 lakh+ villages.
🏛️
Government Scheme Stack
Seamless integration with PM-KISAN, KCC, PMFBY, e-NAM, and NABARD. Our platform auto-enrolls eligible farmers in relevant schemes, unlocking the full benefit stack and improving their creditworthiness simultaneously.
🤝
Blended Risk Architecture
Risk is distributed across farmer (PMFBY premium), lender (priority sector lending quota), insurer (parametric payout), and state (NABARD credit guarantee). No single party bears catastrophic risk — enabling rates as low as 4% p.a.

Risk Architecture

Layered insurance:
removing catastrophic risk

We partner with a dedicated agri-insurer offering parametric triggers — payouts based on verified data events (IMD rainfall below threshold, satellite crop failure) rather than lengthy loss assessment. The insurance wrapper acts as collateral replacement.

🌿
Tier 1: Monsoon Shield
Triggered when district rainfall falls below 75% of LTA. Covers 60% of loan principal automatically.
TRIGGER: IMD District Rainfall Index
🛰️
Tier 2: Satellite Yield Cover
NDVI-based crop yield estimation. If yield drops 30%+ vs. 3-year average, 80% loan coverage kicks in.
TRIGGER: Sentinel-2 NDVI ≤ threshold
🌪️
Tier 3: Catastrophe Cover
For declared natural disasters: floods, cyclones, hail. Full loan waiver plus ₹10,000 emergency grant, backed by state reinsurance pool.
TRIGGER: State / NDRF disaster declaration
🏛️ Government Scheme Integration
PMFBY
World's largest crop insurance. Premium: 1.5–2% for farmers. Centre + state split the rest. KrishiSetu auto-enrolls eligible farmers at loan origination.
PM-KISAN
₹6,000/year in 3 installments to 11+ crore farmers. KrishiSetu uses PM-KISAN disbursement as repayment reliability signal and bridges inter-installment gaps.
KCC (4% rate)
Our loans flow through KCC infrastructure: ₹10.05 lakh crore disbursed, 7.72 crore accounts. We enrich KCC with crop-specific repayment scheduling.
NABARD CGTMSE
Credit guarantee fund covers up to 75% of loan principal for small farmers — reducing lender risk and enabling lower interest rates without collateral.
e-NAM / MSP
Repayment auto-deducted at e-NAM sale. MSP guarantee as floor price allows pre-determining repayment capacity at loan sanction itself.

Investment Thesis

Why This Works —
and Why Now

A structured assessment of technical, regulatory, market, and operational feasibility — with honest risks and concrete mitigations.

A
Technical Feasibility
All data sources (IMD, ISRO Bhuvan, AgriStack, PMFBY) are publicly accessible APIs. No proprietary satellite required.
A−
Regulatory Feasibility
NBFC-AA framework + Account Aggregator stack directly enables the consent-based data model KrishiSetu requires.
B+
Market Timing
AgriStack rollout (2025–27) + Budget 2025 agri-credit mandate creates a narrow 18-month first-mover window.
B
Execution Risk
Mitra network training and rural connectivity are solvable but require sustained on-ground investment in Year 1.
🛰️
Data Infrastructure Is Already Built
IMD publishes 1km² rainfall grids daily. ISRO Bhuvan provides NDVI (crop health) rasters free to NBFC partners. AgriStack's land records API is live in 14 states. No proprietary data collection required — KrishiSetu is a layer on top of India's existing agricultural data stack.
✓ Zero data capex
⚖️
Regulatory Tailwinds Are Explicit
RBI's NBFC-Account Aggregator framework (2021) legally enables farmers to consent-share their PM-KISAN, land, and crop records with lenders. The Priority Sector Lending mandate means banks are legally required to lend to agriculture — KrishiSetu gives them a defensible credit model to do so profitably.
✓ Regulatory pull, not push
💡
ML Model Is Trainable From Day One
NABARD's GIS agri-database + 10 years of KCC disbursement/NPA data (available under RTI + MoU) provides ~2 million labelled training examples for crop-default prediction before a single loan is disbursed. The model improves with each repayment cycle — creating a data flywheel competitors cannot replicate without the loan history.
✓ Cold-start solved
📵
Connectivity Constraint Is Mitigated
Offline-first mobile app with local SQLite sync. IVR fallback in 12 regional languages for feature phone users. KrishiMitra submits applications from district CSC kiosks. 4G coverage in target pilot districts (Vidarbha, Bundelkhand) already exceeds 68% as of 2024 TRAI data.
⚠ Managed, not eliminated

Why KrishiSetu Is Defensible
🔄
Data Flywheel
Every repaid loan adds a verified crop-cycle data point. After 500K loans, the model's predictive accuracy becomes an asset no competitor can replicate without the same repayment history.
🤝
Trust Network Effects
KrishiMitras embedded in villages create social proof and word-of-mouth that outperforms any digital acquisition. A farmer who receives a fair loan becomes a recruiter for 5–10 neighbours.
🏛️
Government Rail Lock-in
MoUs with CSC-SPV, NABARD, and IMD create institutional dependencies that make switching to a competitor complex and politically costly for state governments.
🌦️
Agronomic IP
Crop-specific default models trained on 10+ years of NABARD GIS data + ISRO NDVI indices. Encoded as proprietary scoring algorithms that are not reverse-engineerable from outputs alone.
💳
Credit History Monopoly
The first agri-credit score a farmer ever receives creates lock-in: their entire credit history lives on KrishiSetu. Switching means starting from zero — a psychological and financial barrier.
📊
B2B Data Moat
Aggregated (anonymised) crop-credit data sold as DaaS to seed companies, fertiliser cos, commodity traders, and reinsurers — a revenue stream that grows without adding borrowers.
